FIELD RESEARCH & VOLUNTEERING

Understanding invisible poverty.

Beijing Hongfeng Volunteer Organization

Conversations with elderly parents who had lost their only child revealed gaps between having resources and having reliable care. I translated these insights into recommendations for medical accompaniment, emergency support and daily care.

32Older adults interviewed
24Households interviewed
130 hIn-depth conversations
100 hActivities before formal interviews
18Community activities attended

What the interviews revealed

30 of 32 had basic material resources but still lacked care support. 26 of 32 lacked a reliable emergency contact. All 32 reported loneliness and difficulties attending medical appointments without accompaniment.

From findings to support

Revised training materials reached 18 volunteers. Services directly benefited 30 families, with an estimated 80 indirect beneficiaries. The organization also welcomed 23 older adults through peer referrals.
